## 💡 What Makes Berrytrade Different?

1. **100% Free & Open-Source**: All 24 strategies are auditable and licensed under the MIT License.
2. **Strict Risk Disclosures**: We do not sell "holy grail" algorithms. All strategies disclose their maximum drawdowns, stop-loss mechanics, and risk categories.
3. **No Hidden Martingale**: High-risk lot-multiplication strategies are quarantined and explicitly labeled `Extreme Risk` with liquidation autopsies.
4. **Reproducible 3-Year Backtests**: Standardized 3-year continuous backtests (2023--2025) under realistic 120ms latency, floating spreads, and $7/lot commissions.
5. **Institutional Portfolio Risk Parity**: Equal Risk Contribution (ERC) and Hierarchical Risk Parity (HRP) models that compress portfolio drawdowns to 1.28%.
