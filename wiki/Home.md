## 💡 What Makes Berrytrade Different?

1. **100% Free & Open-Source**: All strategies are auditable and licensed under the MIT License.
2. **Strict Risk Disclosures**: We do not sell "holy grail" algorithms. All strategies disclose their maximum drawdowns, stop-loss mechanics, and risk categories.
3. **No Hidden Martingale**: High-risk lot-multiplication strategies are quarantined and explicitly labeled `Extreme Risk`.
4. **Reproducible Backtests**: Standardized backtest reports with modeled spreads and commissions are provided for every strategy.
